8. WHAT WENT WRONG IN THIS WINDOW, AND WHY
   a. Late stretches come from the box being busy, not from menus.
      09:11:14 - the kiosk's service resume started Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr and Byparr again (Byparr's scope:
      35.9 s CPU, 794 MB memory peak, 770 MB swap peak). The TV look then ran 1686 / 209 and 1633 / 210 late for
      two reports, and too_heavy latched (the "This CRT look is too heavy for smooth video" notice) - against a
      shipped look. After the game ended the containers started again: 956 / 194 in the next half minute.
      A third stretch, about 09:32:15-09:32:55 (1744 / 108), has no cause identified; too_heavy latched again.
      I first blamed the Settings menu and then the guide; the clean runs in section 6 show neither is the cause.
      Changed afterwards (tube-crt-renderer a681cf5, reviewed): too_heavy now needs more than half the presents late over
      30 s, or more than 5% late in each of ten consecutive minutes. A look that really is too heavy measured 77%
      late (TV on a full-screen film, window 5). NOT yet re-run on hardware with that change.
   b. NOT a Tube defect, found here: on main, one "Page flip wait timed out" leaves the kiosk failing nearly
      every later page flip until it restarts.
      09:16:06.726 "Page flip wait timed out" (85 s after the game ended, during the container restart), then
      "Page flip failed (ret=-16, Device or resource busy)" on nearly every frame - "98 failures in last 100
      frames" - until the test kiosk was restarted at 09:21. Every number taken between 09:16:06 and 09:21 was
      discarded. The production journal has the same pattern: about 162,000 printed ret=-16 lines (one failure in
      ten is printed) from 2026-11-14 23:00 to 2026-11-15 09:00 and again 16:00-17:00, with exactly one wait
      timeout. Cause as read from platform/frame_presenter.cpp: the timed-out wait leaves a flip pending, the
      next flip is EBUSY and falls back to SetCrtc, and the late flip event then satisfies the NEXT flip's wait,
      so the loop stays one event behind. Proposed to the owner as its own task; not fixed in these branches.
