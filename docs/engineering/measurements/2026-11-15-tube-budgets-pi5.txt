Tube renderer on the Pi 5: GPU prices per look after each optimisation, and what each trim buys
================================================================================================
Plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-tube-crt-renderer.md, Task 15 (budgets per look). Continues
2026-11-15-tube-programs-pi5.txt (the first window: binary 4ad8223, before any optimisation).
Box: magicpi5 (Raspberry Pi 5, 2 GB), V3D 7.1.10.2, Mesa 25.0.7, Modern TV 1920x1080 @ 60 Hz, picture 1440x1080,
a 960x720 H.264 playlist video playing. Test-only preview builds run from /home/magic/tube-preview; the production
kiosk stopped for each window and restored afterwards (binary hash and settings.json unchanged each time).
"ms" is the kiosk's own GPU timers (MDB_TUBE_GPU_TIMING=1), a 10 s average; "frames" is frames drawn in those 10 s
(300 = 30 fps; 200 = every third vblank = 20 fps).

RESULT (after window 5; windows 1-4 below led to the afterglow rule, section 8 measures it)
- The practical ceiling for 30 fps is about 23.5 ms of tube GPU time per frame; the cliff to 20 fps is near 24.5 ms.
- Two changes that keep the picture: (1) values that depend only on uniforms are computed once a frame on the CPU
  (commits b778afa..9e65ff9): 1.3-1.9 ms per look, no pixel over 1/255; (2) a wide glow is blurred on targets half the
  quarter's size (5b11aa0): the blur 2.3-4.05 ms -> 0.3-0.7 ms, at most 1/255 on every shipped look.
- With both, measured: PVM 19.1, Monitor 17.1, Trinitron 22.6, Arcade 22.9 ms - these four hold 30 fps (timers off:
  Trinitron 5-11 late of ~1795 presents a minute, Arcade 6-13). TV 24.5 and Everything 24.4 ms still draw at 20 fps.
- Three measured ways to bring TV and Everything under the ceiling (section 6):
    the cheap beam on the rich outputs   TV 22.6  Everything 22.7 ms   timers off: 4 and 1-5 late of ~1800
    afterglow off                        TV 22.6  Everything 22.5 ms   timers off: 5-9 and 2 late of ~1800
    chroma bleed off                     TV 23.0  Everything 22.8 ms   (pacing not run)
  The cheap beam changes pixels by up to 9-33/255 (on-Pi tool), so the choice is the owner's.

6. Reading
   - Glow was the expensive effect: off, it saved 3.9 ms on TV; its blur alone cost 3.6-4.05 ms at the size TV,
     Everything and Arcade use against 1.64 ms at glow size 10. Hence change (2).
   - Afterglow nets about 1.8 ms (the history pass 2.4 ms, less an output that is about 0.7 ms cheaper when it draws
     into the history than to the screen). Replacing the copy pass by a framebuffer blit was measured with the on-Pi
     tool at a net 0-0.3 ms and was not done.
   - The output pass is 10.0-10.6 ms on the signal looks, 12.6 ms on Arcade and 13.9-14.8 ms on the raster looks; its
     time follows its per-pixel instruction count (see the correction in 2026-11-15-tube-programs-pi5.txt).
