RESULT
- The engine draws: all six looks, no GL error, no build failure, no refused frame. A paused (still) screen rests at 0 % CPU.
- All 19 programs draw through their real passes on BOTH scene-decode paths; worst first real draw 44.0 ms; the build
  marker is gone after each build.
- A forced shader hang under the production watchdog is caught: one SIGABRT, one restart, then Enhanced until reboot.
- PVM and Monitor hold 30 fps (22.4 and 19.9 ms of GPU per frame). Trinitron, TV, Arcade and Everything do NOT:
  27-31 ms per frame, drawn at 20 fps. The rich budget (23.1 ms) is exceeded by the OUTPUT pass: 15.5-16.3 ms on the
  raster looks against 11.2 ms priced in the spike.
- Observed: three raster output programs compile in V3D's 2-thread mode (441-479 instructions, 43-47 max
  temporaries); every other output compiles in 4-thread mode (412-495 instructions, at most 32 max temporaries).
  See sections 3-4.
- CORRECTION (2026-11-15, later the same day): the 2-thread mode is NOT why the raster looks miss the budget. A
  pixel-identical rewrite that put all ten output programs in 4-thread mode measured SLOWER with the on-Pi tool
  (magic_dingus_box_cpp/scripts/dev/tube_shaderstat, --time 150 minimum ms): out_rich_raster_ag 474 inst / 2
  threads 15.77 ms -> 523 / 4 threads 16.75 ms (about 6 % slower); out_rich_raster 15.98 -> 17.30 ms. The output
  pass's time follows its per-pixel instruction count (exp/sqrt/divide most of all). Moving the uniform-only work to
  the CPU (src/tube/tube_derived.h) cut out_rich_raster_ag 474 -> 431 instructions, still 2 threads, 15.7 -> 14.2 ms
  (-9.6 %). Figures and method: docs/engineering/tube-perf-2026-11-15.md. These are
  off-screen tool timings beside the running kiosk, relative, not the kiosk's GPU timers; on those estimates the
  four raster looks still miss 30 fps.

4. Reading of section 3
   out_rich_raster, out_rich_raster_ag and out_rich_raster_only_ag are the only programs reported "2 threads" (43-47
   max temporaries; every 4-thread output has 32 or fewer). out_rich_raster_ag is the output of Trinitron, TV and
   Everything, and out_rich_raster_only_ag of Arcade: the four looks that miss 30 fps.
   CORRECTION (2026-11-15, later the same day): this section first read the 2-thread mode as the cause ("at 2 threads
   the same pixels take about twice the shader time"). Measured since, it is not: with the tool above, a
   pixel-identical 4-thread rewrite of out_rich_raster_ag (474 -> 523 instructions) took 15.77 -> 16.75 ms, about 6 %
   SLOWER, and 474/2t against 523/4t and 417/2t against 445/4t were each slower at 4 threads by roughly the extra
   instructions. The output pass's time follows its per-pixel instruction count (exp/sqrt/divide), not the thread
   count; the raster outputs cost more than out_rich_signal_ag (11.3 ms in section 6) for their two extra
   beam-weighted line taps and their extra instructions. See docs/engineering/tube-perf-2026-11-15.md "The finding" and "CPU-derived values".
