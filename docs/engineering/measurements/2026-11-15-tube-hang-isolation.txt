Raw output: 2026-11-15-tube-round3-hang.txt, 2026-11-15-tube-round3-hang_narrow.txt.

Program: fullscreen.vert + v2_signal.frag (the round-2 signal pass). Define sets tried:

  [SIG_TONE SIG_CHROMA SIG_CONV SIG_JITTER SCENE_HW_DECODE]   HANG   (the suspect; reproduced first try)
  [SIG_TONE            SIG_CONV SIG_JITTER SCENE_HW_DECODE]   builds (chroma bleed removed)
  [SIG_TONE SIG_CHROMA          SIG_JITTER SCENE_HW_DECODE]   builds (convergence removed)
  [SIG_TONE SIG_CHROMA SIG_CONV            SCENE_HW_DECODE]   HANG   (jitter removed)
  [         SIG_CHROMA SIG_CONV SIG_JITTER SCENE_HW_DECODE]   HANG   (tone removed)
  [SIG_TONE SIG_CHROMA SIG_CONV SIG_JITTER                ]   HANG   (shader decode instead of hardware)

For comparison, round 2 built and drew these without trouble (the tv, arcade and everything looks):
  [SIG_TONE SIG_SHARP SIG_CHROMA SIG_CONV SIG_JITTER SCENE_HW_DECODE]

FINDING
The V3D compiler hangs (more than nine minutes observed in round 2; killed after about 20 s here) on the
signal program whenever SIG_CHROMA and SIG_CONV are both compiled in and SIG_SHARP is not. Tone, jitter and
the decode method do not matter. 4 hangs in 4 attempts; the two controls built. The construct was not
narrowed further than the define set.

WHAT THE RENDERER PLAN MUST DO WITH IT
- The fixed program table must not contain a signal program with chroma bleed and convergence but without
  the sharpness code. Any program that has both must compile the sharpness path too and switch it off by its
  uniform when the row is neutral (that is the variant known to build).
- This is one known bad combination, not a proof that the others are safe: every program in the table is
  still compiled and drawn on hardware (Task 14) before the feature is switched on, under the build guard.
- A hang is silent: no GL error, no log line, one core at 100%. Only the guard and the watchdog can see it.
