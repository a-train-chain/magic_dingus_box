NOTES
- Timers matter. With the GPU timers on, EVERY configuration, even B at 8.85 ms, locked to 30 fps. With
  them off, 12.3 ms of lean output held 60 fps. Round 2's "30 fps at 10 ms" readings were the timers, and
  the 8 ms budget inferred from round 1 was too low.
- The present hold works: with it, every rich reference landed on an even 33.3 ms, 99.4 % or better, up to
  24.1 ms of GPU.
- The probe stalls a frame each time it runs. Its GPU cost is negligible, but PVM-lean with it was late on
  8.5 % of frames, one in twelve, which is exactly the probe's cadence (mipmap + read-back every 12th frame).
  As implemented it cannot be in the lean class.
- "Motion" is one block here (noise, hum, flicker, the rolling band and purity compiled together): +2.55 ms,
  and with it the pass fell to 96.8 %. Round 1 priced hum, flicker and the band at about 0 and noise at
  1.4 ms, so the block is probably mostly noise; it was not split in this run.
- Two readings are unexplained and are not relied on: "B + beam, exact" alone at 17.92 ms (the same beam
  inside PVM-lean costs 0.7 ms more than the cheap one), and "PVM-lean, cheap beam, no probe" at 78.8 % on
  time, although a strict superset of it (the next configuration, with the displacements added) held
  99.53 %. Something outside the tube disturbed that run (it was the first after a matrix start; max
  interval 283 ms). Task 15's ten-minute runs decide PVM-lean properly.
- Today's Enhanced engine costs 11.69 ms and holds 98.7 % at 60 fps on this box: the engine customers
  already have is itself just under the 99 % line at 1080p.
- Not measured: a 720p output; a Pi 4B; the lean pass at 1920x1080 (full-screen films); a ten-minute soak.

HOW THE DECISION WAS REACHED (controller's reading of the rules in the plan)
- lean_budget_ms: the largest lean total whose timers-off run held 99 % is "bend + cheap + tone + displace"
  (8.85 + 0.47 + 1.31 + 0.61 + 1.03 = 12.27 ms, a sum of measured increments), minus 0.5 = 11.8.
- The lean set is that configuration itself: it is the richest one that held. Motion (+2.55, 96.8 %) and
  the probe (a stalled frame each run) are out. So, beyond the seven rows that are always rich (glow,
  afterglow, sharpness, convergence, chroma bleed, raster, colour drift), these also force 30 fps for now:
  noise, hum, flicker, the rolling band, purity (the motion block), and ABL and breathing (the probe).
  Splitting the motion block (hum and flicker are probably free) and a probe that does not stall are
  follow-up measurements for Task 15; either would move rows back to 60 fps.
- rich_budget_ms: all five rich references held; the largest is 24.12, minus 1.0 = 23.1. Trinitron (23.76)
  and tv half + cheap (24.12) sit just over it and take the first step of the rich ladder in Task 15.
- pvm_lean: no. PVM-lean needs the probe (PVM has ABL 20 and breathing 15), and its own run without the
  probe did not hold. PVM and Monitor therefore keep Sound Factory's values and draw at 30 fps, where they
  measured 99.7 % and 99.97 %. No shipped look is retuned to be lean; the 60 fps class is for a custom mix
  that leaves every 30 fps row neutral.
